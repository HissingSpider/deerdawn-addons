# Reading list

Keep the books and articles you mean to read in one place, and let your AI
keep it up to date as you talk.

Mention a book you want to read and it goes on the list. Say you started it,
or finished it and what you thought, and the item moves along with a rating
and your notes. Ask what to read next, what you read this year, or what you
thought of a book, and your AI answers from your own list.

## What it stores

One collection, items: title, author, kind (book, article, paper or other),
link, status (want, reading or done), a rating from 1 to 5, start and finish
dates, your notes, and tags.

## Privacy

Your list is yours. In a team workspace nobody else can read it, workspace
owners included.
